## How to use this map

- The current release tickets below are actionable/provisional according to INDEX.md. A contribution column means a **narrow partial contribution**, never full closure of a retained objective. Existing confirmed audit/preview work stays confirmed only at its original scope/source.
- Each `U-O…` or `U-S…` row names the next unresolved bounded obligation. These are **provisional follow-up decisions**, not newly confirmed or implementation-ready tickets. Before activating one, inspect its named current consumer and original source, research the consequential decision, and freeze the smallest implementation/qualification ticket with observable acceptance. If its contract is missing, the investigation must deliver the exact consumer/input/authority contract, required fixture, checks and decision; dependent work remains provisional.
- Every row retains separate personal and enterprise obligations. A local OWNER, injected tenant, same-host witness or synthetic sink cannot qualify organization identity/custody, outside administration or production effects. The Codex profile is public-owner-only; no private task-memory or live organization support is silently added.
- Only `U-S-linguistic` is owner-deferred. After-release sequencing of the other rows is prioritization, not a new deferral or removal. Full product completion requires their original acceptance, not just the next leaf written here.
- Missing original detail remains RQ001's named gap. This map covers the known maintained baseline, not unknown missing content. No unconditional full-original-detail completeness claim is supported.
